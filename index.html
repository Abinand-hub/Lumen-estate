Questions

Answers before you ask.

Cannot find yours? Send us a message and we will reply within one working day.

Can I visit a home before I decide?

Yes. We arrange viewings seven days a week, and you are welcome to bring family or an adviser.

How much deposit do I need?

Most buyers put down between 10% and 25%. The budget planner above shows how the deposit changes your monthly payment.

Do you help with financing?

We introduce you to lenders we trust and help you compare offers. You choose who to borrow from.

What happens after I move in?

Every new home comes with twelve months of snagging support. Tell us about any issue and we send someone to fix it.

Can I buy before the building is finished?

For developments still under construction, you can reserve a home with a refundable deposit and follow progress with site visits.

Tell us what you are looking for.
